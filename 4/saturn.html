Saturn

Overview

Saturn is the sixth planet from the Sun and the second largest in the solar system. Like Jupiter, it is a gas giant made mostly of hydrogen and helium. Saturn is best known for its spectacular rings, which are made of countless chunks of ice and rock ranging from tiny grains to pieces as big as houses. Saturn also has more moons than any other planet, including Titan, the only moon in the solar system with a thick atmosphere.

Interesting Facts

  • Saturn is less dense than water, so it would float in a bathtub big enough to hold it.
  • Saturn's rings stretch about 175,000 miles from the planet, but the main rings are only about 30 feet thick.

Quick Facts

  • Type: Gas giant
  • Distance from Earth: 749.1 million-1.03 billion miles
  • Size: 74,898 miles in diameter
  • Discovery: Known since ancient times; Galileo first observed it through a telescope in 1610, and Christiaan Huygens identified its rings in 1655
