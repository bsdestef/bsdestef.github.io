Whirlpool Galaxy (M51)

Overview

The Whirlpool Galaxy is a spiral galaxy that faces Earth almost head-on, giving us a clear view of its sweeping arms. A smaller companion galaxy, NGC 5195, is tugging on one of its arms. This gravitational pull is thought to have strengthened the Whirlpool's spiral shape and triggered bursts of new star formation. The pink patches along its arms are clouds of glowing gas where new stars are being born.

Interesting Facts

  • The Whirlpool was the first galaxy ever recognized to have a spiral shape, spotted by Lord Rosse in 1845.
  • Its spiral arms are busy star factories, where gas is squeezed together to form new stars.

Quick Facts

  • Type: Spiral galaxy
  • Distance from Earth: About 31 million light-years
  • Size: About 60,000 light-years across
  • Discovery: Discovered by Charles Messier in 1773
