M87*

Overview

M87* is a supermassive black hole at the center of the giant galaxy Messier 87. It is one of the biggest black holes known, with about 6.5 billion times the mass of the Sun. M87* is very active: it blasts out a jet of particles moving at nearly the speed of light that stretches about 5,000 light-years into space. In 2019, it became the first black hole ever photographed, appearing as a dark shadow surrounded by a glowing ring.

Interesting Facts

  • M87* was the first black hole ever imaged, with the picture released on April 10, 2019.
  • The image was captured by the Event Horizon Telescope, which linked radio dishes around the world to act like one telescope the size of Earth.

Quick Facts

  • Type: Supermassive black hole
  • Distance from Earth: About 55 million light-years
  • Size: About 6.5 billion times the mass of the Sun
  • Discovery: Hubble confirmed a massive black hole in M87 in 1994; the Event Horizon Telescope imaged it in 2019
